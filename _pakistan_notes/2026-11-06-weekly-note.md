**The week in one line:** inflation fell to 9.6% in October and oil dropped sharply, but the FBR missed its four-month target by Rs124bn.

## What changed this week

- **Inflation eased to 9.6% in October** from 10.3% in September, [according to the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics](https://www.pbs.gov.pk/wp-content/uploads/2020/07/Monthly-Review-October2026.pdf). Prices still rose 1.1% in the month, led by chicken, wheat and flour, while tomatoes, onions and potatoes got cheaper. Urban core inflation fell to 7.8% from 8.6%.
- **The tax collector fell short.** The FBR collected Rs974bn in October against a target of Rs1.132tr, taking July–October to Rs4.06tr, [Rs124bn below target](https://profit.pakistantoday.com.pk/2026/10/31/fbr-collects-rs974-billion-in-october-rs406-trillion-in-four-months-of-fy27) and its first shortfall of the fiscal year. Income tax was the main gap.
- **Oil swung hard.** Brent tumbled midweek as US election results [eased fears of a wider Iran war](https://www.brecorder.com/news/40447531/psx-buying-continues-kse-100-gains-nearly-2), then [rebounded to $98.29 a barrel](https://lufkindailynews.com/news_reuters/business/brent-crude-jumps-settles-up-over-3-after-tanker-attacks/article_061e302b-1abf-5f1f-8ab0-318bc6f4fbd3.html) on 5 Nov after tanker attacks. The government [cut petrol and diesel prices](https://mettisglobal.news/Petrol-price-slashed-by-Rs941-diesel-by-Rs556-per-litre-64825) from 6 Nov.
- **Stocks rallied.** The KSE-100 rose 1.96% on 5 Nov to close at 175,380, back above where it ended 2025. The rupee firmed to 276.67 per dollar.
- **Reserves inched up again.** The SBP's own reserves rose $39m in the week to 30 Oct, to $21.56bn, leaving $1.44bn to go to the $23bn goal for end-December.

## Where things stand

| | Latest | For comparison |
|---|---|---|
| CPI inflation | 9.6% (Oct) | 10.3% in Sep |
| Core inflation, urban | 7.8% (Oct) | 8.6% in Sep |
| Policy rate | 12.00% | +2.4 pp above headline inflation |
| SBP reserves | $21.56bn (30 Oct) | goal: $23bn by end-Dec |
| FBR collection, Jul–Oct | Rs4.06tr | target about Rs4.18tr |
| Current account, Jul–Sep | −$874m | −$755m a year earlier |

**The squeeze on prices is loosening, slowly.** Headline inflation has now fallen for two months, from 11.1% in August to 9.6% in October, and core inflation in cities dropped to 7.8%. With the policy rate at 12.00%, real interest rates are 2.4 points above headline inflation and 4.2 points above urban core, a wider cushion than a month ago. But the weekly basket of essentials was still 11.32% dearer than a year earlier in the week to 29 Oct, and wholesale prices were up 13.8%, so the pressure has not gone.

**Revenue is the new worry.** After meeting its July–September target, the FBR has now collected 26.6% of its Rs15,264bn goal for the year with a third of the year gone. A shortfall this early makes it harder to hold the budget path that has kept the primary balance in surplus.

**The outside world helped this week.** Cheaper oil, if it lasts, would ease both the import bill and fuel prices at home. The current account was in deficit by $874m in July–September; October's figure, due later this month, will show whether high oil prices widened it further.

Whether oil holds near this week's lower levels; whether the FBR can close its Rs124bn gap; and whether SBP reserves keep moving towards the $23bn goal.
