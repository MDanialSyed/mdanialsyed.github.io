About these numbers

Source: Pakistan pulls together the latest releases from the State Bank of Pakistan, the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, the Finance Ministry and the Federal Board of Revenue, plus market closes for stocks, the rupee and oil. Most figures are read from those institutions' own releases or from news reports of them, and every row links to where it came from. Figures are updated as new releases come out and checked before they go live.

Pakistan's fiscal year runs from July to June, so FY27 is the year that began in July 2026. Year-to-date changes, real interest rates and gaps to targets are worked out from the figures shown. The scorecard's statuses and the summaries of forecasters' views are this site's own reading. Nothing here is investment advice.
