About these numbers

Source: Pakistan brings together the latest figures from the State Bank of Pakistan, the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics, the Finance Ministry and the Federal Board of Revenue, with market closes for stocks, the rupee and oil, and outlooks from the IMF and World Bank.

Figures are moving to official data feeds one at a time. A figure marked is downloaded from the publisher's own data service, archived exactly as received and calculated by code, and shows its series code, calculation, download time and file fingerprint. A figure marked is interim: read from the release, or a news report of it, and linked to that source. Every figure is checked before it goes live.

Pakistan's fiscal year runs from July to June, so FY27 is the year that began in July 2026. Year-to-date changes, real interest rates and gaps to targets are worked out from the figures shown. The scorecard's statuses and the summaries of forecasters' views are this site's own reading. Nothing here is investment advice.
